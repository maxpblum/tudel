  The sandbox's API file has errors. I'd fix STRUDEL_API_M3.md before batches 2
  and 3, or point them at VERIFIED_FACTS.md:
  - .add(7) after note(…): it doesn't transpose. It logs a warning, which fails
    the verify gate. This breaks the sandbox's canon and pitch-drift examples.
    The forms that work are .add(note(7)) and .add(n(4)).
  - Chord spellings: Cmaj7, Csus4 and Csus2 play silence. The spellings that
    work are C^7/CM7 and Csus. There is no working sus2 symbol, so sus2 is
    taught as the sus4 a fifth up, or written out as notes.
  - Slash chords: they can't go inside chord("…"). They're taught as inversions
    or as a separate bass part.
  - invert: it does exist in Strudel, but it flips a rhythm pattern. Melodic
    inversion is taught as .mul(-1).
  - detune: it only works on supersaw, where it sets the total spread in
    semitones.
  - Default gain: it is 0.8, not 1.
